## Pressure interpretation (latest user correction)
Pressure is multidimensional: spatial precision and clearance, timing, click/action density, prediction/phase/choice, continuous carried-state control, and sustained demand/recovery. None is a proxy for all the others. Equal-frequency small flaps are not intrinsically monotonous or bad; a low-frequency narrow/precise passage can still be demanding. Stage0 may establish sustained precision, stage1 add predictive redirection, stage2 combine time and state demands without requiring every measured series to rise monotonically.

The frozen author-informed controller exposes input frequency/intervals, attained excursions, sampled clearance and actual phase/contact state. These do not measure human anticipation, subjective pressure, perceived fun, or true input-timing/correctable-state tolerance windows. Those remain unknown unless separately tested. No sampled distance is called a control margin or safe interval. Prior near-flat curves motivate inspecting causes, not a narrow rejection criterion.
