## Strongest remaining pressure/rhythm gap

Beyond the separately diagnosed lower-laser failure, the stronger next question is variety and continuity of handoff–answer–recovery relationships. B2 realizes one common closing-gate motif well enough to observe its geometry, but the ending still concentrates that motif and some springs retain short base bypass contours. A useful next relation should create a distinct carried-state receipt or controlled recovery with its own spatial/timing demand, while keeping optional routes and preserving the new occupied-closing evidence. It need not raise flap frequency or force a contact.

Treat a body-backed recovery, optional spring receipt and sustained closing-gate control as different possible shapes. Prefer investigating one specific source-supported relation in the existing bounded panel over increasing the test matrix, imposing family/contact quotas, narrowing all gaps, or chasing a target success percentage. The eventual preregistered full-map checks still need to establish complete final-stage coverage.
