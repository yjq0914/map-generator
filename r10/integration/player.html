What this player does

The map file and all history stay in this browser. No upload, server, network request, or publication is required. Open this HTML next to its vendor/flappy-runtime.js file. Every collider is drawn from the canonical runtime at the actual current tick and full current player state. Dashed inactive bodies are non-colliding at that instant, and can activate later. Circular lethal bodies are filled full disks; the renderer does not invent windows inside them. Gold guides are authored targets, not proof of a playable route. Experimental full-map composition is separate from any narrowly qualified original combo recipe.
